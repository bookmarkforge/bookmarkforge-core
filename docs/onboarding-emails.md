# BookmarkForge onboarding emails

Canonical copy for the onboarding sequence. These emails are provider-neutral: the delivery system can render the Markdown sections as HTML or plain text.

## Email 1 — Welcome

**Subject:** Smart search is free on BookmarkForge — and Free works on any device

**Preview:** Raindrop charges yearly for smart search. BookmarkForge includes it free, with no device cap on Free.

Welcome to BookmarkForge.

Two things are worth knowing before you import your Pocket library:

- **Smart search is included free.** Search by meaning as well as words. Raindrop charges yearly for that feature.
- **Free has no device cap.** Use BookmarkForge on any device. Your vault remains local and encrypted; there is no limit of one device on the Free plan.

Export your Pocket library, open the importer, and drop in the file. Titles, tags and original save dates come with it.

[Import my Pocket library](https://bookmarkforge.com/app)

## Email 2 — Migration help

**Subject:** Move your Pocket library without losing tags or dates

**Preview:** Import your Pocket export locally, then search it for free on any device.

Pocket's export is an HTML file. BookmarkForge reads the links, titles, tags and original save dates directly on your device — nothing needs to be uploaded.

After the import, **smart search is included free** so you can find an idea by meaning, not only by an exact word. **Raindrop charges yearly for smart search.**

You can also open the same Free vault on any device: **there is no device cap on Free**. Each device keeps its local vault unless you choose to sync or export.

[Open the migration guide](https://bookmarkforge.com/pocket-alternative)

## Email 3 — First win

**Subject:** Find one saved idea in seconds

**Preview:** Try smart search, included free, from any device.

Your first quick win: search for an idea, not a title. Try a phrase such as “articles about making remote work calmer.” BookmarkForge's smart search looks at meaning as well as words.

That feature is **included free** — unlike Raindrop, which charges yearly for it. And you can use the Free plan on **any device with no device cap**.

Save a link from the browser extension, then search it from the app. Your vault stays local and encrypted.

[Open BookmarkForge](https://bookmarkforge.com/app)

## Copy guardrails

- Keep the smart-search message and the no-device-cap message in the first screenful of every email.
- Say **“Raindrop charges yearly for smart search”**, not that Raindrop charges for all search.
- Say **“no device cap on Free”**, not “unlimited sync”: devices can have separate local vaults unless the user explicitly configures sync.
- Do not describe the Free plan as having “one device”.
