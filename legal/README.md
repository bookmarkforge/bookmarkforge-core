# BookmarkForge — private legal workspace

This directory is private operational material. It is intentionally excluded from Git and from the public-repository export.

Do not store production secrets, full payment credentials, vault passwords, recovery phrases, or unredacted customer data here. Store signed contracts and provider evidence in the approved encrypted document repository, then record only the reference, status, signer, and review date in `REGISTRO.md`.

## Structure

- `REGISTRO.md` — controlled signature and evidence log.
- `proveedores/` — one folder per vendor; keep the signed DPA, terms, SCC/transfer evidence, subprocessor list, and a short data-flow note here.
- `plantillas/` — unsigned templates and checklists only.
- `evidencias/` — redacted screenshots, checkout role confirmation, invoices, and provider correspondence.
- `revisiones/` — dated legal/privacy review notes.

## Operating rules

1. Use the legal entity named in the current privacy policy and seller account.
2. Never mark a DPA as signed from a public web page alone; record the exact contract or click-acceptance evidence.
3. Confirm whether each provider is a controller, processor, independent recipient, or merely a subprocessor for each processing activity.
4. Confirm international transfer safeguards before production use.
5. Review this register at least annually and whenever a provider, hosting region, product feature, or legal entity changes.
6. The register is an operational draft, not legal advice. A Spanish privacy lawyer or gestor must validate it before launch.
