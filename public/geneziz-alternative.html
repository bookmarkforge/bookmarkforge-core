Move from Geneziz in 3 Steps

Geneziz keeps your library as plain markdown files on your disk — the easiest possible export. BookmarkForge imports those same files and adds encrypted storage, smart search and an AI layer on top. BookmarkForge Pro is $59 early bird ($79 regular), a single payment that includes 12 months of updates; v1 keeps working forever.

  1. Locate your Geneziz library

    Geneziz stores each bookmark as a markdown file in the folder you chose at setup. Nothing to export — your library is already plain files on disk.

  2. Open the BookmarkForge importer

    Open BookmarkForge — no account needed — and click Open Importer on the dashboard. It accepts .md, .html, .json, .csv and .zip files.

  3. Drop in your files

    Select your Geneziz folder or its .zip archive. BookmarkForge imports titles, content and metadata, then encrypts everything on your device. Keep your markdown files as a backup — they remain readable anywhere.
