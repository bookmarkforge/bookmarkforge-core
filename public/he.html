תמיכה — כמה מהר אתם עונים?

Free: Community help via Help Center + GitHub Discussions (best effort). Pro: email 48h business days. Vault is encrypted — we cannot see your data, so we never ask for your password or recovery phrase. See Support SLA.

מה אם שכחתי את סיסמת הכספת?

We cannot recover it. When you create a vault we show 24-word recovery phrase once — write it on paper. Without it, no one (including us) can open the vault. See BF-E101.
