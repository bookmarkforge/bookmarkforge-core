Move from Instapaper in 3 Steps

Instapaper is a solid read-later service, but Premium is a monthly subscription and your library lives on its servers. BookmarkForge gives you the same save-and-read-later loop with smart search included free — encrypted on your own device. BookmarkForge Pro is $59 early bird ($79 regular), a single payment that includes 12 months of updates; v1 keeps working forever.

  1. Export your Instapaper library

    In Instapaper open Settings → Export to download your bookmarks, or use your browser's bookmark export if you also saved there.

  2. Open the BookmarkForge importer

    Open BookmarkForge — no account needed — and click Open Importer on the dashboard. It accepts .csv, .html, .json, .md and .zip files.

  3. Import and organize

    BookmarkForge imports your links with titles and metadata, then you can add notes, tags and folders — everything encrypted locally. Smart search by meaning is free, where Instapaper gates its best features behind Premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookmarkForge a good Instapaper alternative?

For private, local-first bookmark management with a one-time price, yes. Instapaper is stronger if you want speed reading, text-to-speech playlists, or Kindle article delivery.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks, smart search and export — forever, no trial. Pro is a one-time payment for lifetime access to v1.

Can I import my Instapaper bookmarks into BookmarkForge?

Instapaper exports bookmarks as CSV or a simple list of URLs. BookmarkForge imports CSV, HTML, Markdown and JSON, so you can bring your archive across.

Does BookmarkForge work on mobile?

Yes — it's a progressive web app that installs on any device and works fully offline.

Which is more private?

BookmarkForge: your vault is encrypted on your device and never uploaded. Instapaper stores your library on its servers to power sync and search.

Does BookmarkForge have AI features?

Yes — smart search by keyword or meaning, summaries and AI chat over your saved content, running locally on your device or with your own API keys. Instapaper focuses on readability and sending articles to Kindle; it has no comparable AI layer.

Is the pricing on this page up to date?

BookmarkForge pricing is current: Free with 500 bookmarks, Pro at $59 early bird for the first 200 licenses then $79, one-time payment including 12 months of updates, and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Instapaper Premium is a separate monthly subscription — check its site for current pricing.
