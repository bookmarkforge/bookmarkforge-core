Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookmarkForge a good Instapaper alternative?

For private, local-first bookmark management with a one-time price, yes. Instapaper is stronger if you want speed reading, text-to-speech playlists, or Kindle article delivery.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks, smart search and export — forever, no trial. Pro is a one-time payment for lifetime access to v1.

Can I import my Instapaper bookmarks into BookmarkForge?

Instapaper exports bookmarks as CSV or a simple list of URLs. BookmarkForge imports CSV, HTML, Markdown and JSON, so you can bring your archive across.

Does BookmarkForge work on mobile?

Yes — it's a progressive web app that installs on any device and works fully offline.

Which is more private?

BookmarkForge: your vault is encrypted on your device and never uploaded. Instapaper stores your library on its servers to power sync and search.
