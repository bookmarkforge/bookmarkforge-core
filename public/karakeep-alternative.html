Move from Karakeep in 4 Steps

Karakeep is a capable self-hosted bookmark manager, but it needs a server, Docker and ongoing maintenance. BookmarkForge gives you the same local-first idea as a zero-setup PWA — encrypted on your device, no server to run. BookmarkForge Pro is $59 early bird ($79 regular), a single payment that includes 12 months of updates; v1 keeps working forever.

  1. Export your Karakeep bookmarks

    Use Karakeep's export feature or its API to get your bookmarks out as JSON/CSV, or fall back to your browser's bookmark export if you saved through the extension.

  2. Open the BookmarkForge importer

    Open BookmarkForge — no account, no Docker — and click Open Importer on the dashboard. It accepts .json, .csv, .html, .md and .zip files.

  3. Import your library

    BookmarkForge maps titles, URLs, tags and notes into your encrypted vault. Lists you curated in Karakeep become tags or folders you can search instantly.

  4. Retire the server (optional)

    Once your library is in BookmarkForge you can stop running the Karakeep container. Your data lives on your devices — keep a BookmarkForge encrypted backup and you no longer depend on a host.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookmarkForge better than Karakeep?

BookmarkForge is better for users who want a simple PWA experience without Docker setup. Karakeep is better for self-hosters who want full control.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks. Pro is a one-time payment of $59 for lifetime access to v1.

Does BookmarkForge require Docker?

No, BookmarkForge is a PWA that works in any modern browser. No Docker or server setup required.

Can I import from Karakeep?

Yes, you can export your bookmarks from Karakeep and import them into BookmarkForge.

Which is more private?

BookmarkForge is more private because your data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync.

Does BookmarkForge have AI features like Karakeep?

Yes — smart search by keyword or meaning, summaries and AI chat over your saved content. It can run models locally on your device or use your own API keys, so your data does not have to go to a third-party server the way Karakeep's optional OpenAI integration does.

Is the pricing on this page up to date?

BookmarkForge pricing is current: Free with 500 bookmarks, Pro at $59 early bird for the first 200 licenses then $79, one-time payment including 12 months of updates, and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Karakeep itself is free open source — you pay for the server and upkeep instead.

Does BookmarkForge support lists and tags like Karakeep?

Yes — folders, tags and smart collections cover what Karakeep calls lists, and smart search by keyword or meaning finds things Karakeep's tag-only browsing would miss.

Is BookmarkForge really free?

The Free tier includes 500 bookmarks, smart search and offline use with no account and no device cap. Pro is a single $59 early-bird payment ($79 regular) covering v1 forever plus 12 months of updates.

Is BookmarkForge open source?

The Core is MIT-licensed open source — you can audit the code yourself. Pro features (sync, unlimited bookmarks, advanced AI) stay in the private repo under the one-time license.
