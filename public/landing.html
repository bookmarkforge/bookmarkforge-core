Privacy Isn't a Feature — It's the Foundation

Every architectural decision puts you in control

End-to-End Encryption

AES-256-GCM with keys derived from your master password via Argon2id. We never see your password or keys.

Local-First Architecture

Your vault lives in IndexedDB on your device. The companion server only handles AI proxy, licensing, and signaling — never your content.

Zero Telemetry by Default

No analytics, no crash reports, no tracking. Optional opt-in for aggregated usage stats and error reporting — each independently controlled.

You Hold the Keys

Device key separation: JWK in localStorage + ciphertexts in IndexedDB. Both required. No master password = no access. No "forgot password" backdoor.

GDPR Compliant

Granular consent (analytics, error reporting, client events). Right to access, rectify, erase, port — all doable locally. ROPA documented.

No Vendor Lock-in

Export your data anytime in open formats (JSON, Markdown, CSV, PDF). Your knowledge stays portable.
