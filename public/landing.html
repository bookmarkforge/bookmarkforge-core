Common Questions

Is BookmarkForge really free?

Yes! The free tier gives you 500 bookmarks, smart search, and all the basics — forever. No trial, no credit card, no catch. Upgrade to Pro only if you want unlimited bookmarks, sync across devices, or AI features.

Do I need to create an account?

Nope! BookmarkForge works instantly in your browser. No sign-up, no email, no password. Just open the app and start saving.

What happens to my data if I stop using it?

Your data stays on your device. You can export everything anytime to Markdown, JSON, CSV, or PDF. No lock-in, no hostage situations.

Is my data really private?

Absolutely. Everything is encrypted on your device with a key only you have. We never see your bookmarks, notes, or passwords. Even we can't read your data.

What if I forget my password?

When you first set up BookmarkForge, we show you a 24-word recovery phrase. Write it down and keep it safe. Without it, no one (including us) can access your vault.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes! BookmarkForge works on any device with a web browser. The free tier works on unlimited devices (each has its own vault). Pro syncs across up to 5 devices.

How do I import my bookmarks from Chrome/Firefox?

Easy! Go to Settings → Import and choose your browser. BookmarkForge will automatically bring in all your bookmarks, folders, and tags.

What payment methods do you accept?

We use Whop for payments: credit card, PayPal, and crypto. We never see your payment details. 30-day money-back guarantee — no questions asked.
