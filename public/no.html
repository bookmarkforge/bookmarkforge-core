Privacy Isn't a Feature — It's the Foundation

Every architectural decision puts you in control

Locked so nobody can read it — not even us

Your library is encrypted with a key derived from your master password. We never see your password or your content.

Stored on your own device

Everything lives on your computer. The companion server only handles licensing, device pairing and relaying your cloud-mode AI questions — never your content.

No tracking by default

No analytics, no crash reports, no tracking. Sharing usage stats or error reports is a separate choice, off by default.

You hold the keys

No master password means no access — there is no "forgot password" backdoor. Your 24-word recovery phrase is the only way in; keep it safe.

Your rights, respected

Access, correct, erase or take your data with you — all doable locally, whenever you want.

No lock-in

Export your data anytime in open formats (JSON, Markdown, CSV, PDF). Your knowledge stays yours and portable.

Common Questions

Is the Free tier really free forever?

Yes. No trial, no subscription. The free tier includes 2,500 bookmarks, full-text & semantic search, knowledge graph, and AI with your own API key (you pay the provider).

What does "Lifetime of v1" mean for Pro?

Pay once, own v1 forever. Includes all v1.x updates (bugfixes + minor features) forever and 12 months of feature updates from purchase. Security updates forever. After month 13 you keep v1 functional, just without new features. Future major versions (v2, v3) are separate products: $89 for new customers, $35 for v1 owners (60% off).

What payment methods & refunds?

Via Whop: credit card, PayPal, crypto. We never see your payment details. 30-day refund — contact bookmarkforge@proton.me.

Support — how fast do you answer?

Free: Community help via Help Center + GitHub Discussions (best effort). Pro: email 48h business days. Vault is encrypted — we cannot see your data, so we never ask for your password or recovery phrase. See Support SLA.

What if I forget my vault password?

We cannot recover it. When you create a vault we show 24-word recovery phrase once — write it on paper. Without it, no one (including us) can open the vault. See BF-E101.
