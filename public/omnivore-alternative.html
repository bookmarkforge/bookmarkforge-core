Frequently Asked Questions

Is Omnivore still available?

Omnivore's hosted service ended in November 2024 after the team joined ElevenLabs. The codebase remains open source and can be self-hosted, but there is no hosted service to sign up for.

Can I move my Omnivore library to BookmarkForge?

If you still have an Omnivore export, it comes as HTML or Markdown files with your highlights and notes. BookmarkForge imports HTML and Markdown, so you can bring that content across.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks, smart search and export — forever, no trial. Pro is a one-time payment for lifetime access to v1.

Do I need to self-host BookmarkForge?

No — it's a progressive web app. Install it from your browser and your encrypted vault lives on your device.

What happened to my Omnivore data?

If you exported before the shutdown or still run a self-hosted instance, your data is safe. Omnivore provided an export window before the hosted service ended.
