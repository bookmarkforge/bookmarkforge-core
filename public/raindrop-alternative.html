Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookmarkForge better than Raindrop.io?

BookmarkForge is better for privacy-conscious users who want local-first storage. Raindrop.io is better for users who want cloud sync and collaboration features.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks. Pro is a one-time payment of $59 for lifetime access to v1.

Does BookmarkForge sync across devices?

Yes, Pro includes P2P sync across up to 5 devices without a central server.

Can I import from Raindrop.io?

Yes, you can export your bookmarks from Raindrop.io and import them into BookmarkForge.

Which is more secure?

BookmarkForge is more secure because your data is encrypted on your device and never leaves it unless you choose to sync.
