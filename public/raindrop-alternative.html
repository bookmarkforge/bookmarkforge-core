Move from Raindrop.io in 4 Steps

Raindrop.io keeps your library on its servers and bills yearly for AI search. BookmarkForge imports your export file in minutes — this time encrypted on your own device. BookmarkForge Pro is $59 early bird ($79 regular), a single payment that includes 12 months of updates; v1 keeps working forever.

  1. Export your Raindrop.io library

    In Raindrop.io open Settings → Import/Export and export your bookmarks. The export contains your links with titles, tags, folders and notes.

  2. Open the BookmarkForge importer

    Open BookmarkForge — no account needed — and click Open Importer on the dashboard. It accepts .html, .json, .csv, .md and .zip files.

  3. Drop in your export

    BookmarkForge detects common bookmark export formats automatically and preserves titles, URLs, tags and folders where the source provides them.

  4. Keep it private

    Everything lands encrypted on your device. Smart search by keyword or meaning is included free — the feature Raindrop reserves for paid plans — and Free works on any device with no device cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookmarkForge better than Raindrop.io?

BookmarkForge is better for privacy-conscious users who want local-first storage. Raindrop.io is better for users who want cloud sync and collaboration features.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks. Pro is a one-time payment of $59 for lifetime access to v1.

Does BookmarkForge sync across devices?

Yes, Pro includes P2P sync across up to 5 devices without a central server.

Can I import from Raindrop.io?

Yes, you can export your bookmarks from Raindrop.io and import them into BookmarkForge.

Which is more secure?

BookmarkForge is more secure because your data is encrypted on your device and never leaves it unless you choose to sync.

Does BookmarkForge have AI features like Raindrop?

Yes — smart search, summaries and an AI chat over your saved content. The difference is where it runs: BookmarkForge can run models locally on your device or use your own API keys (BYOK), while Raindrop's AI features process your data on its cloud servers.

Is the pricing on this page up to date?

BookmarkForge pricing is current: Free with 500 bookmarks, Pro at $59 early bird for the first 200 licenses then $79, one-time payment including 12 months of updates, and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Third-party prices change — check each service's own page for the latest figures.

Does BookmarkForge have collections and folders like Raindrop?

Yes — nested folders, tags and smart views organize your library, and everything stays searchable by keyword or meaning instead of browsing folders one by one.

What about mobile apps and browser extensions?

BookmarkForge is a progressive web app: install it on any device from the browser and it works fully offline. There is no separate mobile app to maintain, and the same encrypted vault syncs on Pro.
