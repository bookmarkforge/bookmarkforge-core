Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookmarkForge a good Readwise alternative?

For saving, organizing and privately searching your own library, yes — BookmarkForge is local-first, encrypted, and Pro is a one-time payment. Readwise is stronger if your workflow centers on Kindle highlights, RSS and newsletters, or team features.

Is BookmarkForge free?

Yes, BookmarkForge has a free tier with 500 bookmarks, smart search and export — forever, no trial. Pro is a one-time payment for lifetime access to v1.

Can I import my Readwise highlights into BookmarkForge?

Readwise exports highlights to CSV and Markdown/JSON formats. BookmarkForge imports JSON, HTML, Markdown and CSV, so you can bring your library across and keep it on your own device.

Does BookmarkForge have spaced repetition?

Yes — Pro includes flashcards with spaced repetition you can generate from your saved articles.

Which is more private?

BookmarkForge: your vault is encrypted on your device and never uploaded. Readwise stores your library on its servers to power sync and search.
