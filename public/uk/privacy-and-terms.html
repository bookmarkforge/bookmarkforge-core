Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the processing described in this policy is BookmarkForge (contact in section 17). For the companion server and license infrastructure, the operator of that deployment acts as controller.

2. Introduction & Scope

BookmarkForge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • No bookmarks or URLs are sent to our servers
  • No notes or documents leave your device
  • No AI prompts or responses are logged by us
  • No browsing history is tracked
  • No personal information is required to use the app
  • No analytics are collected by default

4. Where Your Data Lives

All your data is stored locally in your browser using:

  • IndexedDB - for bookmarks, documents, and settings
  • Secure Storage - for encrypted keys and passwords
  • Service Worker Cache - for offline functionality

5. Encryption

Your data is encrypted using:

  • AES-GCM-256 encryption for all stored content
  • Argon2id (memory-hard KDF) for password-derived keys
  • Keys are derived and stored locally in your browser

6. Content Security Policy (CSP) Reports

BookmarkForge uses Content Security Policy (CSP) headers to prevent cross-site scripting and data injection attacks. If your browser blocks a resource because of these headers, it automatically sends a brief technical violation report to the same origin that served the application (the /csp-report endpoint). The operator's collector keeps only:

  • The origin of the blocked resource (path, query and fragment are stripped; never your personal data)
  • The CSP directive that was violated
  • The origin of the page where the violation occurred
  • A one-way salted hash of your IP address, used solely for rate limiting -- your IP is never stored

7. AI Providers

When you use AI features:

  • Local AI (WebLLM): Runs entirely in your browser. No data leaves your device.
  • Cloud AI (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.): Your prompts are sent directly to the provider. We do not intercept, store, or log these requests. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

8. Optional Error Reporting (Opt-In Only)

You may choose to enable error reporting in Settings. If enabled:

  • We receive anonymous technical error logs when the app crashes
  • We NEVER receive: your bookmarks, text, URLs, prompts, or any personal content
  • Only technical stack traces and device information are sent
  • You can disable this at any time

9. PWA Bookmarklet & Share Target

BookmarkForge offers two ways to save content from your browser:

  • Bookmarklet: Drag the "Save to Forge" button to your bookmarks bar. Click it on any page to save instantly.
  • PWA Share Target: On mobile, use your browser's native Share menu to send links to BookmarkForge.

10. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts - for typography (fonts are cached locally)
  • AI Providers - Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, etc. (only if you configure them)

11. Your Rights (GDPR)

BookmarkForge is designed so that we never receive your personal data, which means most GDPR obligations fall away by design. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you retain the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15): All your data is on your device. Use the Export feature to obtain a complete, machine-readable copy at any time.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): Edit your bookmarks, notes, and documents directly in the app.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): Use the built-in data removal (Settings -> Danger Zone / Nuclear Forget). This permanently deletes all local data, including encrypted keys and recovery data. We hold no copy.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): Exports are open formats (JSON, Markdown, HTML) usable in other applications.
  • Right to object and restrict processing (Arts. 18/21): All optional processing (cloud AI, telemetry, error reporting) is strictly opt-in and can be disabled at any time in Settings.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7): Disabling optional features in Settings withdraws consent immediately and stops all associated processing.

15. Children's Privacy
