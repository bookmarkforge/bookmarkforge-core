The Core source code of BookmarkForge is free software under the MIT License
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). **The brand is not.** Pro components are proprietary
and are not included in this public export. This
policy explains how the name, logo and other brand assets may and may not be
used. It applies to everyone, including forks and derivatives.

## The Marks

- The name and wordmark **BookmarkForge**
- The BookmarkForge logo and icon
- Confusable variants of the name, and the reserved domains
  `bookmarkforgeapp.com` and any official social handles

These are either registered trademarks or are claimed as such, and they are
**not** covered by the MIT grant (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)).

## What is allowed without permission

You may use the Marks to refer to the software truthfully and neutrally
("nominative fair use"):

- Stating that your product is compatible with BookmarkForge ("Imports
  BookmarkForge `.bmf` kits").
- Reviewing, critiquing, teaching or writing about BookmarkForge.
- Referring to the upstream project in a changelog or bug report
  ("based on BookmarkForge 1.0.2").

## What always requires permission

- Using "BookmarkForge" or a confusingly similar name **as the name of your
  product, project, fork, company or service**.
- Using the logo or icon, in whole or in part.
- Registering domains, subdomains, social handles or app-store entries
  containing the Marks (e.g. `bookmarkforge.app`, `bf-vault.com`).
- Implying endorsement, affiliation or certification by the BookmarkForge
  project ("BookmarkForge Pro by <you>", "official", "certified").

## Forks and derivatives

You are free to fork the MIT-licensed Core under the MIT terms. A fork must:

1. Use its **own name and own logo** — never the Marks, never
   "BookmarkForge" plus a modifier ("BookmarkForge X", "BF Forge" for a
   competing product).
2. Include a clear disclaimer, e.g. *"not affiliated with or endorsed by
   BookmarkForge"*.
3. Keep the upstream copyright and license notices intact.

## How to request permission

Write to **legal@bookmarkforgeapp.com** with a short description of who you are
and how you want to use the Marks. We aim to answer within 10 business days
and are generally happy to grant free licenses for community, educational and
non-commercial uses.

## Enforcement

Unpermitted use of the Marks — especially product naming, domain squatting or
brand impersonation — will be enforced against, including via platform
takedown procedures (GitHub DMCA, domain and app-store complaints) where
appropriate.
